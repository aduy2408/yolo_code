# Research Decision Log

One row is required for every experiment. Link the hypothesis, manifest, result artifacts, and failure analysis.

## Decision vocabulary

- `reject`: mechanism falsified or unsafe;
- `revise`: one mechanism change justified by observed failure;
- `replicate`: promising but underpowered;
- `promote`: passed the declared gates;
- `infrastructure-investigation`: no scientific conclusion is possible.
